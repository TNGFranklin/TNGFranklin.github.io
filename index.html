How I work

My work follows the same four steps every time. First I learn how the system is meant to work. Then I look for the point where it does not work that way. Then I prove the problem is real. Then I write it down clearly, so someone else can repeat my steps and fix it.

At my last job this meant testing client networks, firewalls, databases, and mobile apps. I also ran fake phishing emails to see how staff would react, and checked systems against the NIST CSF, ISO 27001, and ITGC standards. Next to that I keep doing research in cryptography and privacy, because many of the best problems sit below the app layer.

Each writeup on this site has the same four parts: what the target was, how I attacked it, the technical detail with real code, and what I got in the end.
